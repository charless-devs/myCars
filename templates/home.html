Six Decades of American Motion

Ford put the world on wheels — and the Mustang put performance within reach of everyone. This collection carries that legacy from the very beginning: the 1965 fastback that started it all, through the legends of the eighties and nineties, to the electrified and supercharged machines of today.

021965 — Today

1965 to the Latest

Every era has its moment. Follow the line from the World's Fair debut to the machines rolling off the line today.

  1. 1965

    The Mustang Is Born

    Unveiled on April 17 at the New York World's Fair, the 289-powered Mustang creates an entirely new class of car.

  2. 1969

    Torino Cobra Jet

    Ford's mid-size muscle answers the drag strip with the 428 Cobra Jet and Ram Air induction.

  3. 1971

    Falcon XY GTHO Phase III

    Australia's most famous homologation special takes the 351 Cleveland to Bathurst victory.

  4. 1985

    RS200 — Group B

    A mid-engined, all-wheel-drive rally weapon built for the most extreme era of motorsport.

  5. 1995

    GT90 Concept

    Quad-turbo V12, carbon fibre body — Ford's "world's fastest supercar" concept pushes design into the future.

  6. 2016

    Ford GT Returns to Le Mans

    Fifty years after the legendary 1-2-3 finish of 1966, the EcoBoost-powered GT wins its class again.

  7. 2021

    Bronco Reborn, Mustang Electrified

    Two icons return at once — the off-road Bronco and the all-electric Mustang Mach-E.

  8. 2024

    Dark Horse & Raptor R

    500 horsepower in the Mustang Dark Horse. 720 in the F-150 Raptor R. The legend keeps growing.

  9. 2026

    The Latest

    Mustang, Ranger, F-150 and Puma carry the blue oval forward — the story is still being written.
